# ads-os

Use this skill when the task involves campaign iteration, creative testing, landing-page friction, or budget prioritization.

## When to Use

- Reviewing paid traffic performance
- Designing creative or landing-page experiments
- Reallocating budget across campaigns or offers
- Diagnosing CPA, CVR, CTR, or ROAS problems

## Output Structure

1. `signal`
   Which metric is actually failing and at what stage.
2. `hypothesis`
   The most likely explanation for the failure.
3. `test`
   The smallest experiment to validate or kill the hypothesis.
4. `budget_rule`
   How spend should be protected, reduced, or increased.
5. `creative_next`
   What angle, format, or message should be tested next.
6. `decision_gate`
   The metric threshold for scaling, holding, or shutting off.

## Procedure

1. Isolate the broken stage of the funnel before suggesting fixes.
2. Prefer one-variable experiments over broad campaign rewrites.
3. Protect spend while learning; do not pay for ambiguity.
4. Link creative changes to landing-page reality and offer quality.
5. Define explicit scale or stop thresholds.

## Do Not

- Treat more budget as a substitute for better signal.
- Change targeting, creative, offer, and landing page at the same time.
- Celebrate click volume without contribution to revenue.
