# bd-os

Use this skill when the task is about finding, qualifying, contacting, or advancing a partner, affiliate network, or business-development opportunity.

## When to Use

- Creating a target-partner list
- Drafting outreach sequences and follow-ups
- Preparing offers, intros, or partner notes
- Diagnosing why a relationship is stalled

## Output Structure

1. `target_fit`
   Why this partner is relevant now.
2. `offer`
   The value proposition for them, not just for us.
3. `outreach_angle`
   The best opener and proof points to use.
4. `follow_up_plan`
   The exact next touches and cadence.
5. `objections`
   Likely resistance and how to answer it.
6. `next_commitment`
   The smallest useful next step to secure.

## Procedure

1. Qualify fit before writing outreach.
2. Tailor the offer to the partner's incentives and operating model.
3. Keep messages short, specific, and easy to respond to.
4. Separate relationship-building from closing asks.
5. Always end with a concrete next commitment.

## Do Not

- Send generic outreach that could apply to anyone.
- Ask for too much before trust exists.
- Let a thread go stale without a documented next touch.
